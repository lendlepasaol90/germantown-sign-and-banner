About Us

Germantown's local, full-service sign company

We help businesses throughout Germantown and the surrounding communities make a strong impression with signage built on local expertise, quality craftsmanship, and genuine customer service.

Who we are

A sign company built around Germantown businesses

Germantown Sign and Banner is a local source for custom signs, banners, vehicle graphics, and visual branding. We work with businesses of every size throughout Germantown and the surrounding communities, delivering signage from concept and design through fabrication, installation, and ongoing maintenance.

Germantown is one of southeastern Wisconsin's most vibrant communities, known for its strong business environment and strategic location within the Milwaukee metropolitan area. As local businesses compete for attention, we believe professional signage plays an increasingly important role in attracting customers and building long-term brand recognition — and we're here to help make that happen.
